# Privacy Policy — Scribble (Annotate & Snip Anywhere)

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

Scribble is a browser extension that lets users draw, highlight, add shapes and
text, and take screen snips on top of any webpage they visit.

## Data collection

Scribble does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or
browsing information. Specifically:

- All drawings, highlights, shapes, and text you create are held only in the
  memory of the current browser tab and are cleared when the page is reloaded
  or closed.
- Screenshots and snips you capture with the extension are processed entirely
  on your own device and are only saved to your computer if you explicitly
  click "Download," or copied to your clipboard if you explicitly click "Copy."
- The extension does not send any data to the developer, to Microsoft/Google,
  or to any third-party server. There are no analytics, tracking scripts, or
  remote network requests of any kind.
- No account, sign-in, or personal information is required or requested.

## Permissions

Scribble requests the following browser permissions, used only as described:

- **activeTab / host permissions** — to display the drawing toolbar and canvas
  on the page you are currently viewing.
- **scripting** — to inject the toolbar and canvas into the current page when
  you activate the extension.
- **downloads** — to save annotated screenshots or snips to your computer when
  you choose to download them.
- **storage** — reserved for locally saving your chosen tool/color/size
  preferences on your own device; this data never leaves your browser.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Any
future update will continue to reflect that Scribble collects no personal
data.
